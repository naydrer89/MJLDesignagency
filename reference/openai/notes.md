# OpenAI — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (white canvas, ink `#000000`, `OpenAI Sans` 400–500).

## What to look at
- Greyscale-only structure: `#F1F1F1` surfaces, `rgba(0,0,0,0.12)` borders, 6px radii on 39 controls.
- Display at 400 weight, 48–72px, `-0.028em`; body 17px with 1.65 line-height.
- Hairline separators and list rows instead of cards wherever possible.
- Colour appears only as data: emerald `#10A37F` for availability, indigo in charts.

## What to steal
- Monochrome focus ring (`0 0 0 2px #000`) — no colour needed for accessibility.
- 56–72px hairline list rows with 18px title / 14px meta.
- Editorial 4/8 split with a sticky label column.

## What not to copy
- The near-total absence of accent colour on consumer pages where affordances need it.
