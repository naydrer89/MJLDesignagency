# Figma — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (true-black bands, `figmaSans` 330–550, `figmaMono` labels).

## What to look at
- Marketing pages alternate hard black and white bands; colour arrives in flat bursts
  (`#0D99FF`, `#A259FF`, `#0ACF83`, `#F24E1E`, `#FFC700`).
- Display sits at weight 400 — scale, not boldness.
- Body at 18px/330 is unusually light; it works because line-height is 1.4 and measure is short.
- Cards are playful objects: 24px radius, 1px hairline, sticker-like hover lift.

## What to steal
- One accent per section, all accents only in brand illustration.
- Mono metadata (`figmaMono`, 12px) for file names and shortcuts.
- Multiplayer presence dots (8px, 2px canvas ring).

## What not to copy
- 330 weight below 16px — it fails contrast on low-DPI screens.
