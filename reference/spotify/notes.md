# Spotify — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (canvas `#121212`, 75 pill elements, `SpotifyMixUI` at 700).

## What to look at
- 700 weight for almost every label; the UI is bold by default.
- Surface ladder `#121212` → `#181818` → `#242424` with zero borders.
- Acid green `#1ED760` only where playback happens.
- Shelves: horizontal rows of 8px-radius cards with cover art as the entire visual.

## What to steal
- Surface steps instead of borders on a dark canvas.
- Circular 48px play button with hover scale and shadow.
- Per-page colour extraction from artwork (top 200px gradient only).

## What not to copy
- Bold 14px body text — it degrades long-form reading.
