# Nike — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (`Nike Futura ND` 76px/500, 40 elements at 30px radius).

## What to look at
- Headlines are 76px at 0.9× line-height, set as image-captions over full-bleed photography.
- Pill buttons at 30px radius, 60px tall on product pages — sport, not luxury.
- Colour = black, white, and one accent (volt `#D4FB00` / orange `#FF5A00`) per campaign.
- Product tiles put the image edge-to-edge; labels sit outside the frame.

## What to steal
- Display type as composition (uppercase, tight, oversized) instead of decoration.
- Hover-swap to a second product image.
- Mega-menu with athlete imagery as the right-hand column.

## What not to copy
- 96px type on informational pages; reserve it for campaign surfaces.
