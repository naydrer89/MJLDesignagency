# Vercel — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (canvas `#FAFAFA`, ink `#171717`, `GeistSans`/`GeistMono`).

## What to look at
- Tracking of `-3.84px` at 64px — the most aggressive negative tracking of the reference set.
- Geometry: 1px bordered rectangles on a strict grid, radii 6px, no shadows at all.
- Mono uppercase labels (11–13px, `+0.06em`) mark every structural section.
- Monochrome primary buttons; blue is reserved for links and states.

## What to steal
- Borders as the only depth device.
- Mono eyebrow + tight grotesque headline as a page-opening pair.
- True black dark mode (`#000000`) so device edges vanish.

## What not to copy
- Extreme tracking below 40px — it destroys legibility.
