# Airbnb — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (188 rounded elements at 20px, `Airbnb Cereal VF`, 700 weights).

## What to look at
- The 20px-radius card with a 1:1 image is the entire information architecture.
- Search is a floating white pill with a level-2 shadow, not an input row.
- Coral `#FF385C` appears only on reserve/search actions; everything else is monochrome.
- Catalogue density: 6 cards per row on desktop, 2.2 on mobile with horizontal scroll.

## What to steal
- Image-first cards with a floating action (heart) and a two-line text block.
- Pill search with hairline segment dividers.
- Sticky booking bar on mobile product pages.

## What not to copy
- 20px radii for everything — the shape must match the content (photos = soft, data = sharp).
