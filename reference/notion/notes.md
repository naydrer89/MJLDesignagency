# Notion — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (84px+ hero, `NotionInter` + `Lyon Text`, warm `#F9F9F8`).

## What to look at
- 96px hero with `-4.6px` tracking, then 14px UI text — a 7× ratio between marketing and product.
- Warm paper neutrals (`#F9F9F8`, `#F1F1EF`, `#E9E9E6`) — no cold grey anywhere.
- 4–12px radii; nothing is a pill except tags and avatars.
- Serif (`Lyon Text`) appears only in editorial and quotes.

## What to steal
- Hover-revealed block affordances (drag handle, `+`) instead of permanent chrome.
- Callout pattern: 10% tinted fill + 3px left border.
- Command palette (`⌘K`) as the primary navigation for power users.

## What not to copy
- 96px type where the page has no single sentence to say.
