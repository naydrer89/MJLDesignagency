# Apple — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg` (1440×900, above the fold), `tokens.json` (computed styles, 4000 elements sampled).

## What to look at
- The navigation is 44px tall, blurred, and disappears into the page — the whole top bar is 12px text.
- One message per viewport: "iPhone Duo / Hello." then the product. No feature list above the fold.
- Type does the hierarchy: 40–80px `SF Pro Display` at 600 against 17px `SF Pro Text`, tracking `-0.374px`
  measured on 34px text.
- Buttons are pills (980px radius measured 34×) in exactly two variants: filled `#0071E3` and 1px outline.

## What to steal
- Section alternation `#FFFFFF` / `#F5F5F7` instead of borders or cards.
- Type scale that steps 17 → 21 → 28 → 40 → 56 → 80 with tracking that tightens as it grows.
- Photography as the only ornament — no gradients, no doodles.

## What not to copy
- The full-bleed scroll length (13 353px) without Apple's content density.
- Product macro shots on pages that don't have a product.
