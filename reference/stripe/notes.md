# Stripe — notes

Capture: `hero.jpg`, `tokens.json` (font `sohne-var`, radii 4–8px, canvas `#FFFFFF`).

## What to look at
- Display weight is **300** at 48–64px — Stripe is the calmest loud page on the web.
- Radii are tiny (4px, 6px, 8px) and borders are `rgba(10,37,64,0.08)`: the page reads as engineering, not marketing.
- One gradient mesh per page (blurple → cyan), always behind a product surface, never behind text.
- Grid is strict: every section starts on the same left edge at 1080/1280px max width.

## What to steal
- Light display weights + `-0.02em` tracking as a substitute for colour.
- Code panels as first-class design objects (`#0A2540` surface, 13px mono).
- Secondary text at `#64748D` — one step, not three.

## What not to copy
- The gradient mesh without a product that deserves it.
