## Brand & Style

Gucci is organised maximalism. A warm cream paper (`#FBF8F1`), the green/red/green web stripe as a 3px rule,
gold for small caps, a serif that flirts with the 1970s, and collage layouts that allow two or three competing
motifs per viewport — held together by a rigid 12-column grid and consistent 4px-baseline spacing. The rule is:
**many ideas, one grid.**

- **Purity & Focus:** narrative over minimalism — but each module still has one job.
- **Dynamic Dual-Mode:** cream for editorial, near-black (`#12100D`) for campaign takeovers.
- **Restrained Tactility:** flat colour, hard edges, stripe and gold used as graphic punctuation.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#0F6B3F`):** Gucci green — the brand stripe, primary CTA in editorial contexts.
- **Secondary (`#E4002B`):** Gucci red — sale, campaign flags, the middle of the web stripe.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A227`):** gold for tracked caps, keylines and insignia.
- **Neutrals:** `#FBF8F1` cream, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#12100D` ink, `#5F5749` secondary, `rgba(18,16,13,0.14)`.
- **Stripe:** green/red/green at 3px height, full-bleed or 48px wide as a badge.

## Typography

- **Serif carries the era:** 26–64px, tight leading (1.03×), near-zero tracking.
- **Sans carries the catalogue:** 11–17px with `+0.02em` to `+0.2em`, uppercase in labels.
- **Bold is allowed here** — 600 in labels is Gucci's exception to the luxury thin-weight rule.
- **Pacing:** body 1.6×, and text blocks may sit directly on imagery inside a cream caption panel.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1600px max, 24px gutters, 56px margins.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768–1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Collage:** 7/5 and 5/7 splits, overlapping caption cards (max 24px offset), full-bleed stripe separators.
- **Mobile:** 2-up product grid, horizontal story rail, sticky "Discover" bar.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Level 0:** cream or black.
- **Level 1:** white cards with 1px hairline, square; caption cards may overlap images by 24px.
- **Level 2:** drawers `#FBF8F1` with a 3px stripe at the top edge — the only "shadow" is that stripe.
- **No blur, no gradients:** flat colour and overlap create depth.

## Shapes

- **Square (0–2px)** on everything; the stripe, not rounding, is the signature.
- **Buttons** are square, 1px bordered or filled green.
- **Badges** are rectangles with a 3px stripe on the left edge.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** green fill, white 11px uppercase `+0.16em`, 46px, square.
- **Secondary:** 1px ink border, transparent fill.
- **Tertiary:** underlined tracked caps with an arrow.

### Product Cards
- 4:5 image, name in sans 14px/600, price 13px, colour swatches as 12px squares (not circles). Hover: second
  image + a 3px stripe appears along the bottom edge.

### Navigation
- 64px cream bar, serif wordmark, 11px tracked caps categories; mega-menu is a full-width cream panel with
  campaign imagery and 4 link columns, separated by hairlines.

### Chips & Stripes
- Chips: square, 1px hairline, 10px/600 `+0.2em`; "New" in green, "Sale" in red.
- Stripe divider: 3px green/red/green, 100% width, used between major story sections.

### Inputs
- 46px, square, cream fill, 1px `rgba(18,16,13,0.2)`, focus 1px green; error copy 11px in `#B3261E`.
