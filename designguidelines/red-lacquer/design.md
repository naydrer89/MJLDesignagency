## Brand & Style

Cartier is lacquer and gold: an ink-black or ivory stage, one saturated red (`#A8112B`), thin gold keylines
(`#C9A227`), a didone serif for names and a widely tracked sans for everything operational. Product photography is
jewellery-lit macro on black; the boutique pages switch to ivory with the same rules. The interface never rushes:
cross-fades of 300–500ms, no bounce, no playful motion.

- **Purity & Focus:** one object per frame, generous negative space, a single red CTA per screen.
- **Dynamic Dual-Mode:** black for campaigns, ivory for commerce and service.
- **Restrained Tactility:** square edges, 1px keylines, gold only as a line or a tiny label.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#A8112B`):** Cartier red — primary CTA, cart count, editorial emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#C9A227`):** gold — keylines, dividers, small caps on dark.
- **Tertiary (`#F7F2E9`):** ivory for text and surfaces on black.
- **Neutrals:** `#000000`, `#141110`, `#FCFAF5`, `#6E6258` secondary, `rgba(20,17,16,0.12)` hairlines.
- **Rule:** red and gold never touch the same element; one accent per frame.

## Typography

- **Serif names, sans facts:** serif for product names and campaign lines; sans for price, availability and forms.
- **Tracking:** `+0.03em` in sans display up to `+0.2em` in 10px labels. Serif stays at 0.
- **No bold:** 500 is the maximum, and only in labels.
- **Pacing:** 1.1× display, 1.65× body; 55-character measure.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1600px max, 28px gutters, 64px margins.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768–1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Showcase:** centred object on a 16:9 black field with the name set below in a 4-column text block.
- **Mobile:** single column, 24px margins, sticky "Book an appointment" bar on boutique pages.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Level 0:** black or ivory.
- **Level 1:** 1px gold or `rgba(20,17,16,0.12)` frame; no fills, no shadows.
- **Level 2:** drawers use `#141110` with a 1px gold top rule and a 70% scrim.
- **No blur, no gradients:** the page is a vitrine, not a surface.

## Shapes

- **Square (0px)** on buttons, cards, inputs; 1px allowed on very large media.
- **Buttons** are 1px bordered rectangles 46px tall or plain underlined text.
- **Avatars / icons** are circles; that is the only curve in the system.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Cartier red fill, white 11px uppercase `+0.15em`, 46px, square.
- **Secondary:** 1px gold border, transparent fill, ivory label.
- **Tertiary:** 11px tracked caps with a 1px underline that slides in on hover.

### Product Cards
- Black field, jewellery macro 1:1, name in serif 17px, collection in 11px tracked caps gold, price in sans 13px.
  Hover: image cross-fades, gold keyline fades in.

### Navigation
- 64px bar, serif wordmark, 11px tracked caps categories, right-side icons; mega-menu as a black panel with
  4 columns and a "Maison" editorial tile.

### Chips
- Square 1px keyline, 10px/500 `+0.2em`; "Neu" in gold, "Bestseller" in ivory.

### Inputs
- 46px, square, transparent with 1px `rgba(247,242,233,0.24)` (dark) / `rgba(20,17,16,0.24)` (light);
  focus turns the line red; helper text 11px `+0.03em`.
