## Brand & Style

Rolex is institutional luxury: a black-and-ivory stage, a single green (`#006039`) and a thin gold line
(`#C8A45C`) that stand in for a crown. Photography is a full-bleed product macro on black; type is a light
grotesque with wide-tracked small caps; corners are square everywhere. Nothing hurries — motion is a slow
cross-fade, scroll reveals are subtle, and the only urgency is a boutique locator.

- **Purity & Focus:** one watch, one story, one call to action per section.
- **Dynamic Dual-Mode:** dark is the signature; light mode is ivory paper (`#F7F4EE`) for editorial and service pages.
- **Restrained Tactility:** square edges, 1px hairlines, gold used at 1px weight — never as a fill.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#006039`):** Rolex green for primary CTAs and the crown mark.
- **Secondary (`#C8A45C`):** gold for hairlines, dividers and small-caps eyebrows.
- **Tertiary (`#E3E0D4`):** cream for surfaces on dark photography.
- **Neutrals:** `#000000` canvas, `#0B1F18` deep green-black panels, `#F4F1E8` ivory text, `#B9C3BA` secondary.
- **Discipline:** at most two colours per view — black/ivory plus one accent.

## Typography

- **Light grotesque at scale:** 300 weight at 40–64px; weight increases only below 20px.
- **Wide tracking on small text:** `+0.14em` at 12px and `+0.18em` at 10px for labels and navigation.
- **No bold headlines:** authority comes from space and contrast, not weight.
- **Pacing:** 1.1× display, 1.6× body; line lengths capped at 60 characters.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1600px max, 32px gutters, 64px page margins on desktop.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768–1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Editorial asymmetry:** models side-by-side at 5/7 or 4/8 column splits, with captions in the narrow column.
- **Mobile:** full-bleed media, 24px margins, sticky "Discover" pill appears after 60% scroll.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Level 0:** pure black or ivory.
- **Level 1:** image on black with a 1px gold hairline frame; no border on full-bleed media.
- **Level 2:** drawers and locators: black `#0B1F18` panel, 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`, no drop shadow.
- **No depth illusions:** no blur, no glass, no shadows — layering is flat and deliberate.

## Shapes

- **Everything is square** (0px radius) except a 2px softening on inputs.
- **Buttons:** rectangular with 1px gold or green border; text-only variant with an arrow.
- **Images:** hard-edged, uncropped by masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Rolex green, square, 13px small-caps label with `+0.14em`, 48px height, padding 0 32px.
- **Secondary:** transparent with 1px `#C8A45C` border, ivory label.
- **Tertiary:** text + long arrow `→`, gold on hover, 300ms ease.

### Watch Cards
- Black canvas, centred product macro, model name at 19px/500, reference in 12px tracked caps, price hidden
  behind "Discover" — never a shop-now button in the hero.

### Navigation
- 64px bar, transparent over hero then solid black; 12px tracked caps; mega-menu as full-width black panel with
  5 columns.

### Chips & Metadata
- Square tags with 1px hairline, 10px/500 `+0.18em`; used for "New", "Certified Pre-Owned".

### Inputs
- Square, 48px, transparent with 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.20)`; focus turns the line gold; label floats at 10px.
