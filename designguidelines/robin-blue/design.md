## Brand & Style

Tiffany sells light: white gallery space, silver-grey structure, a serif wordmark and one unmistakable colour —
Tiffany Blue (`#0ABAB5`). The blue is treated as a jewel: it appears on the box, the primary CTA, and nowhere
else. Photography is macro jewellery on white or on soft grey; type is a light serif for names and a tracked
sans for everything transactional.

- **Purity & Focus:** one piece per frame; the blue box is allowed to be the hero.
- **Dynamic Dual-Mode:** white default, near-black for evening campaigns.
- **Restrained Tactility:** 0–2px radii, 1px hairlines, thin silver rules, no shadows.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#0ABAB5`):** Tiffany Blue — primary CTA, box illustrations, active states. Text on it is
  `#00201F`, never white (contrast).
- **Secondary (`#81D8D0`):** the lighter heritage blue for tints and hover fills.
- **Tertiary (`#141414`):** ink for buttons in editorial and gift-guide contexts.
- **Neutrals:** `#FFFFFF`, `#F4F4F2`, `#EBEBE8`, `#5F5F5F` secondary, `#B3B3B3` silver lines.
- **Rule:** never place blue type on white below 15px — use ink and keep blue for fills and rules.

## Typography

- **Serif for the object:** 25–56px, regular weight, `+0.01em` — slightly airy, never tight.
- **Sans for the transaction:** 11–17px with `+0.02em` to `+0.18em`.
- **No bold display:** 500 max, used only in labels.
- **Pacing:** display 1.1×, body 1.65×; 55-character measure.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1440px max, 28px gutters, 64px margins.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768–1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Gallery rhythm:** centred single object, caption block beneath in a 6-column measure, 96px of air around it.
- **Mobile:** one column, 24px margins, horizontal gift rail, sticky "Add to bag" bar on product pages.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Level 0:** white or near-black.
- **Level 1:** 1px `rgba(0,0,0,0.10)` or silver `#B3B3B3` frames; no fills.
- **Level 2:** cart drawer: white with a 1px rule and a Tiffany Blue top edge (2px); a 40% black scrim behind.
- **No blur, no gradients** — the blue box is the only "material".

## Shapes

- **Corners:** 0–2px on everything; the blue box is the only object allowed a perfect square.
- **Buttons:** 2px radius rectangles, 46px tall, or underlined text links.
- **Imagery:** hard-edged; product cut-outs on white.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Tiffany Blue fill, `#00201F` 11px uppercase `+0.14em`, 46px, 2px radius.
- **Secondary:** 1px ink border, transparent fill.
- **Tertiary:** underlined tracked caps with a right chevron.

### Product Cards
- White ground, cut-out 1:1, name in serif 17px, collection in 11px tracked silver, price 13px sans.
  Hover: a 1px blue rule appears under the name and a second image fades in.

### Navigation
- 64px white bar, serif wordmark centred, 11px tracked categories, right-side search/account/bag; mega-menu is
  white with 4 columns and a blue gift-guide tile.

### Chips & Badges
- Square 1px hairline chips, 10px/500 `+0.18em`; "Engravable" in blue outline, "New" in ink.

### Inputs
- 46px, 2px radius, 1px `rgba(0,0,0,0.20)`, focus 1px `#0ABAB5` + `0 0 0 3px rgba(10,186,181,0.18)`; helper 11px.
