## Brand & Style

Hermès is craft documented like a museum: warm cream paper (`#F6F1E7`), leather brown ink, saddle-stitch
hairlines, a serif for storytelling and the maison's orange (`#F37021`) reserved for the single most important
element on a page. Product photography is still-life, evenly lit, often on the cream ground itself; type is set
with the calm of a catalogue raisonné.

- **Purity & Focus:** the object and its making. Copy explains craft, not features.
- **Dynamic Dual-Mode:** light cream is the signature; dark mode is a deep leather brown, never black.
- **Restrained Tactility:** square edges, 1px rules, no shadows — the only "material" is paper.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#F37021`):** Hermès orange — one per page, usually the CTA or the cart badge.
- **Secondary (`#8B5E3C`):** leather brown for accents, icons and editorial rules.
- **Tertiary (`#1F4D3A`):** deep green used in seasonal and equestrian modules.
- **Neutrals:** `#F6F1E7` cream, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#2B2118` ink, `#6B5C4A` secondary, `rgba(43,33,24,0.14)` hairlines.
- No pure black and no pure grey — every neutral is warm.

## Typography

- **Serif for the maison:** didone-adjacent headings at 28–60px, regular weight, near-zero tracking.
- **Sans for utility:** 11–18px with slight positive tracking; labels in uppercase at `+0.12em`.
- **Line length:** 55–65 characters; generous 1.6–1.7× leading.
- **Hierarchy:** serif (story) vs sans (transaction) is the primary distinction — not size.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1440px max, 32px gutters, 64px margins.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768–1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Catalogue rhythm:** image left / text right at 7/5, full-bleed intermissions between chapters.
- **Mobile:** single column, 20px margins, horizontal "collections" scroller with visible page dots.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Level 0:** cream paper.
- **Level 1:** white card with 1px hairline, no radius, no shadow.
- **Level 2:** drawer/cart slides over a `rgba(43,33,24,0.4)` scrim; the panel itself is cream with a 1px rule.
- **No glass, no gradients:** depth is paper over paper.

## Shapes

- **Everything square.** 0px on buttons, cards, inputs.
- **Buttons** are bordered rectangles 44px tall, or underlined text.
- **Imagery** is hard-edged; no circular masks except the account icon.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** orange fill, white 11px uppercase `+0.12em`, 44px, square; used once per view.
- **Secondary:** 1px brown border, transparent fill, ink label.
- **Tertiary:** underlined link with a right arrow, 300ms underline offset change.

### Product Cards
- Cream ground, product still-life 4:5, name in serif 18px, material in 13px sans `#6B5C4A`, price in 15px
  medium. Hover: image cross-fades to an alternate angle over 400ms.

### Navigation
- 56px bar, serif wordmark left, 11px tracked caps categories, search/cart right; mega-menu on cream with
  three columns and a small editorial image.

### Chips
- Square tags with 1px hairline, 10px/500 `+0.16em`; "Nouveauté" uses the leather brown, never orange.

### Inputs
- 44px, square, cream fill with 1px `rgba(43,33,24,0.24)`; focus darkens to ink at 1px; error rule in `#B3261E`.
