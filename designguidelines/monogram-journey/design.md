## Brand & Style

Louis Vuitton turns a monogram into a navigational system: deep brown-black canvas (`#1B1A17`), gold hairlines
(`#C8A35A`), a serif for campaign storytelling and a wide-tracked sans for everything transactional. Pages are
built as **journeys** — full-bleed campaign film, then a horizontal collection rail, then editorial product
stories — with the monogram pattern used as texture, never as a background for text.

- **Purity & Focus:** travel and craft as narrative; every module is a chapter.
- **Dynamic Dual-Mode:** dark is default (boutique), light ivory (`#FBF8F1`) for catalogue and service pages.
- **Restrained Tactility:** square geometry, 1px gold rules, slow 400–600ms image transitions.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#542E12`):** the house brown for filled buttons and the monogram ground.
- **Secondary (`#C8A35A`):** gold — hairlines, active underlines, small caps emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#A02020`):** the deep red of the web stripe, for alerts and limited editions.
- **Neutrals:** `#000000`, `#1B1A17`, `#24221E`, `#F4EFE4` ivory, `#C2B9A6` secondary.
- **Pattern usage:** monogram at 6–10% opacity behind editorial blocks only.

## Typography

- **Serif for campaigns:** 25–56px, regular weight, zero or `+0.01em` tracking.
- **Sans for commerce:** 11–17px with `+0.02em` to `+0.18em`; uppercase labels are the norm.
- **Never bold above 500** — the brand is thin, spaced and confident.
- **Pacing:** display 1.1×, body 1.6×; text width capped at 58 characters.

## Layout & Spacing

- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1680px max, 28px gutters, 64px margins.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile `< 768px`, Tablet `768–1024px`, Desktop `> 1024px`.
- **Journey rail:** horizontal snap carousel, 3.2 cards visible on desktop, 1.15 on mobile.
- **Mobile:** full-bleed media, sticky bottom bar with "Discover" + wishlist, burger opens a full-screen menu.

## Elevation & Depth

- **Level 0:** black or ivory.
- **Level 1:** 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.16)` or `rgba(27,26,23,0.12)` frames; no fills.
- **Level 2:** the cart drawer is `#1B1A17` with a 1px gold top rule, no shadow.
- **No glass or gradients** except the product's own campaign imagery.

## Shapes

- **Square everywhere** (0–2px). Pills are reserved for the "LV" initials badge only.
- **Buttons** are 1px bordered rectangles, 46px tall.
- **Media** is hard-edged with optional thin gold keyline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** house brown fill, ivory 11px uppercase `+0.14em`, square, 46px height.
- **Secondary:** 1px gold border, transparent fill, ivory/gold label.
- **Tertiary:** underlined link with `→`, gold underline that animates from 0 to 100% in 300ms.

### Product Rail
- Cards: 4:5 imagery, name in 14px sans, price 13px, "New" in 10px gold tracked caps; hover reveals a
  quick-view bar that slides up 40px over 400ms.

### Navigation
- 60px bar, serif wordmark centred, 11px tracked caps left, icons right; mega-menu as a full-width dark panel
  with 4 columns and one campaign image.

### Chips
- Square, 1px hairline, 10px/500 `+0.18em`; gold for "Exclusive", red for "Sold out".

### Inputs
- 46px, square, transparent with 1px `rgba(244,239,228,0.24)`; focus gold 1px; label 10px tracked caps.
